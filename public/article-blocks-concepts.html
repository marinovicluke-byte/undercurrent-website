19 looks across five blocks, on BRAND.md

Every frame is the live article page's own CSS around copy lifted from live UC articles and run through the real transform, so what you see is what ships. A look is one class on the article: change BLOCK_LOOKS in app/blog/[slug]/page.js. Each block follows BRAND.md: one blue, a blue ramp for data, never red or green, Satoshi and Switzer, rounded tiles. The page around them is still the September design.

The scrape rule holds in every look: an ordered list stays an <ol>, a table stays a <table>, the Quick Answer sentence is a <p><strong> first, phone and desktop are CSS only, nothing hidden or duplicated, nothing behind a tap or a script.
