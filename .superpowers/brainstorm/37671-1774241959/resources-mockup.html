Design Notes

  • Card grid — Responsive 3-column on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile. Cards lift on hover with shadow depth.
  • Card images — Placeholder with subtle wave animation inside each. When articles go live, these become thumbnail images or custom illustrations.
  • Coming Soon overlay — Semi-transparent overlay with frosted glass on each card image. Removed per-card as content goes live.
  • Filter pills — Category filters (All, Guides, Articles, Playbooks, Tools). These will filter cards when content is live.
  • Ocean background — Same flowing canvas animation as the case studies page, tying both pages together.
  • Not in navbar — Page is accessible via footer links and internal article links only. Google indexes it through the sitemap.
  • SEO structure — Each article will get its own /resources/[slug] page with Article schema, canonical, and meta tags. The listing page gets its own structured data too.
