Which album style feels right?

Think about how users will browse your case studies once they're live

A

Classic Book Flip

A single open book/album in the center of the page. Click the right edge to flip to the next page, left edge to go back. Each "spread" (two pages) shows one case study. Pages curl and fold over with a 3D CSS perspective animation — like turning a real page.

Best for: immersive, premium feel. Works great with 4-8 case studies.

B

Stacked Card Flip

Case studies as large cards stacked on top of each other. Click or swipe to flip the top card away (like swiping through a stack of photos). Each card flips/rotates as it leaves, revealing the next one underneath. More modern, mobile-friendly.

Best for: mobile-first, quick browsing. Feels like a portfolio deck.

C

Full-Page Album with Tab Edges

The entire page IS the album. Dark background, centered content area styled like an open album/scrapbook. Visible tab edges on the right side (like dividers in a binder) let you jump to specific case studies. Clicking a tab flips all pages to that study with a cascading page-turn animation.

Best for: maximum visual impact, unique experience. The whole page feels like opening a portfolio.
