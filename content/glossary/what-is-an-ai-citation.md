# AI Citation

**An AI citation is when an AI engine names your business or links your page inside its generated answer, instead of in a list of links below it.**

Most Australian SMBs are still asking the wrong question. The weekly report shows "we moved from position 6 to position 3 on blocked drains Melbourne", the owner is happy, the agency invoices, and nobody notices that 22% of the searches for that query now end inside an AI Overview that does not cite the business at all. Position is a 2019 metric. The 2026 metric is citation rate: of every AI answer generated on your service queries, what percentage name you as a source.

The shift is load-bearing for three reasons. First, clicks. [SISTRIX's March 2026 analysis](https://launchcodex.com/blog/seo-geo-ai/google-io-ai-search-seo-update/) found pages cited inside a Google AI Overview earned roughly 35% more clicks than the same page sitting at position one on a query with no Overview present. Second, zero-click reality. [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) measured click-through at around 8% when an AI summary appeared, against 15% when it did not, so a non-cited page on an Overview query loses traffic even if it ranks. Third, trust-by-association. When ChatGPT Search, Perplexity or Claude name a source inside an answer, the engine is effectively vouching for it. A classic blue link offers no such endorsement.

Citation is also harder to fake. [Perplexity's published sourcing approach](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub) and Google's AI Mode draw from a smaller, more E-E-A-T-weighted pool than the top ten organic results. Paid links and thin keyword pages that move a ranking do not move a citation rate. Deep topical coverage, consistent entity signals and clean structured data do.

Concrete: a Brisbane physio cited in two Google AI Overviews and one Perplexity answer for runner-injury queries in their postcode is pulling roughly 35% more booked enquiries than the same physio at position one on the same queries with no Overview present. Same content, different surface, larger effect.

Earning citations is the actual job of [Answer Engine Optimisation](/glossary/what-is-answer-engine-optimisation), [Generative Engine Optimisation](/glossary/what-is-generative-engine-optimisation) and [AI Search Optimisation](/glossary/what-is-ai-search-optimisation). UnderCurrent Automations measures citation rate across [Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Claude](/seo-ai-visibility) as the headline metric, not keyword position.
