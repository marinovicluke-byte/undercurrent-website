# How to Choose the Best AEO Agency in Australia (2026)

> **Quick Answer:** The best AEO agency in Australia isn't the one with the slickest logo. It's the one whose monthly work you can point to: tracked AI prompts, real content shipped, schema in place, and a citation report you can actually read. Most "best AEO agency" lists you'll find were written by an agency on the list. This guide wasn't. Read the proposal, not the brand.

If you run a small business in Australia and you've started getting pitches for "AEO" or "answer engine optimisation", you're not imagining the noise. AEO is one of the fastest-growing service lines in Australian digital marketing, and a fair chunk of the agencies selling it shipped their first AEO project in the last 6 months. Plenty of "best AEO agency in Australia" roundups are floating around, and almost all of them were [published by an agency that made its own list](https://digitalnomadshq.com.au/blog/best-aeo-answer-engine-optimisation-agencies/). ## What Does an AEO Agency Actually Do?

**An AEO agency's job is to make your business the answer an AI assistant gives, not just a link buried on page two.** AEO is short for answer engine optimisation. Answer engine optimisation is the practice of getting a business named, described and quoted inside the answers tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and [Google's AI Overviews](/blog/ai-search-vs-traditional-search-australia-2026) hand back to people. GEO is short for generative engine optimisation. Generative engine optimisation means the [same work aimed at generative chat tools](/blog/what-is-answer-engine-optimisation), and people use the two terms almost interchangeably. Where classic SEO chased ten blue links, AEO chases the one paragraph the model reads aloud. In practice the work is unglamorous: writing pages that answer real questions in the first sentence, adding schema markup so machines can parse who you are, filling entity gaps (your services, your suburbs, your proof), and earning citations from sources the models trust. None of it is magic. Google's own guidance still comes back to [helpful, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data), and [no special tricks for AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features). A good agency just does that on purpose, every month, and shows you the receipts.

## What Should the Best AEO Agency in Australia Deliver Each Month?

**The best AEO agency in Australia ships work you can see and a report that ties it to outcomes.** A monthly AEO report is a document that lists what content went live, what schema changed, and which AI assistants now name you, compared with last month. Concretely, each month you should get [new content built around real buyer questions](/blog/what-is-answer-engine-optimisation) (expect at least 2,000 words of genuinely new pages or substantial rewrites), schema and entity changes, a [citation check against named prompts](/blog/what-is-ai-search-optimisation-australia), and next month's plan. The point of the report is so a sceptical business partner can read it and see exactly what their money bought. Here's the shape of a monthly report worth actually paying for:

```
MONTHLY AEO REPORT - what a real one shows you
- prompts tracked:       the 12 to 20 buyer questions you actually care about
- citation status:       which AI assistants name you, on which prompts, vs last month
- content shipped:       URLs published this month + the prompt each one targets
- schema + entity work:  what markup changed, which "about" facts were added
- next month's plan:     the 3 to 5 prompts you're chasing next and why
```

If a proposal can't describe a deliverable in that much detail, it isn't a plan, it's a holding pattern. Ask for last quarter's report for a client like you, and [a frank comparison of two or three agencies](/blog/au-seo-agencies-ai-search-audit), before you sign anything.

## How Does an AEO Retainer Differ From an SEO Retainer in Australia?

**An AEO retainer is a monthly engagement aimed at getting your business quoted by AI assistants, while an SEO retainer aims at ranking in Google's blue links.** SEO leans on keywords, backlinks and page speed; AEO leans on clear answers, schema, entity coverage and citations. The reporting is the giveaway: SEO hands you rank-tracker screenshots, AEO hands you a prompt-by-prompt citation log. Plenty of [Australian agencies now run both](/blog/au-seo-agencies-ai-search-audit), which is fine, as long as the AEO half is itemised, not buried in "content". Here's the contrast:

| A typical SEO retainer | An AEO retainer |
|---|---|
| Targets ten blue links on Google | Targets being quoted by AI assistants |
| Levers: keywords, backlinks, page speed | Levers: clear answers, schema, entities, citations |
| Reports rank-tracker screenshots | Reports a prompt-by-prompt citation log |
| Costs roughly $1,500 to $3,000 a month | Costs roughly $3,000 to $5,000 a month |

If your new "AEO" line item has the same deliverables as your old SEO line item, you're paying twice for one job.

## What Are the Red Flags in an AEO Proposal?

**The biggest red flag in an AEO proposal is a guaranteed result, because nobody controls what a model says.** If an agency promises a "#1 spot in ChatGPT" or a fixed ranking inside 3 months, that's a claim they can't substantiate, and the [ACCC](https://www.accc.gov.au/business) is fairly clear that businesses shouldn't make claims they can't back up. Other warning signs: no monthly deliverable you could photograph; "AEO" priced identically to a [generic SEO package](/blog/ai-search-vs-traditional-search-australia-2026); no mention of schema, entities or [a citation report](/blog/seo-audit-self-check-australia); a "proprietary AI ranking score" with no method behind it; and slide after slide about how big AI search is, with nothing about what they'll do for you. Google's own [search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) are blunt about manipulative tactics, so be wary of anyone selling a shortcut. One quieter flag: an agency that won't show you a real client's recent report. Good agencies are itching to. A buyer's guide that won't show its working has the same problem an agency does, which is why ours does.

## How Much Should an AEO Retainer Cost an Australian Small Business?

**A fair AEO retainer for an Australian small business sits between $3,000 and $5,000 a month, with simpler scopes lower and competitive niches higher.** That's real money: more than [2,500,000 actively trading businesses](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/business-indicators/counts-australian-businesses-including-entries-and-exits/latest-release) operate in Australia, and the [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/starting-registering-or-closing-a-business/running-your-own-business/supporting-your-small-business) and the government's [own business portal](https://business.gov.au/online-and-digital) both peg most of them as small and running lean. So price it like a hire, not a punt. Below roughly $2,000 a month you're usually getting a content writer with a new label, not [a real AEO programme](/blog/what-is-ai-search-optimisation-australia). Above $5,000 you should be getting a senior strategist, not just more pages. Whatever the number, tie it to the report: prompts tracked, content shipped, citation movement. A useful sanity check before you sign: get the price broken into writing, schema, prompt tracking and reporting, not lumped together as "content", so you can see what each dollar actually does. If you can't see the work, the price is wrong no matter what it is. Pay for output you can point at, not for activity you have to take on faith.

## Specialist or Full-Service: Which Kind of AEO Agency Do You Need?

**Pick a specialist if AEO is the whole point of the engagement, and a full-service agency only if its AEO line is itemised and run by someone who's done this for 12 months or more.** A small specialist who lives in answer engines all day usually moves faster than a generalist juggling 30 other retainers. The trade-off: a specialist may not touch your ads or your [website build](/website-design), so you carry the coordination. To tell whether any agency is doing AEO or just renaming SEO, [ask three things](/blog/au-seo-agencies-ai-search-audit): show me a recent citation report, show me the schema you shipped last month, and show me three prompts you're chasing next quarter. Thirty minutes of that tells you more than any pitch deck. A real AEO team answers fast and specifically; a rebadged SEO team changes the subject to backlinks. If you're in Melbourne, Sydney or Brisbane you've got plenty of both to compare, so make them prove it before you commit a cent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is AEO worth it for a small business in Australia?

AEO is worth it for an Australian small business when buyers are already asking AI assistants about your service and competitors are starting to show up in those answers. If that's happening, every month you sit out, someone else becomes the default answer. If customers still find you through word of mouth and Google Maps, it can wait. The honest test: open ChatGPT or Perplexity, ask the question a customer would, and see whose name comes back.

### Can a small business do AEO without hiring an agency?

A small business can do meaningful AEO without an agency if someone on the team can write clear answers, add schema markup, and check the AI assistants monthly. The work is more discipline than secret sauce, and Google's [structured data guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/faqpage) is public and free to follow. What an agency buys you is consistency and speed. If nobody internally has the hours, DIY AEO tends to start strong then quietly stop, which is worse than never starting.

### How long before AEO shows results?

AEO usually shows early movement in 8 to 12 weeks and meaningful citation share in 4 to 6 months, depending on your niche and how much content already exists. AI assistants re-crawl and re-rank on their own clock, so progress is lumpy, not linear. A good agency tracks named prompts from month one so you can see drift before it becomes a trend. If someone promises results in 30 days, they're misleading you or counting something that doesn't matter.

### What's the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO and GEO describe the same work. AEO, answer engine optimisation, is the broad term for getting cited inside AI-generated answers, including Google's AI Overviews. GEO, generative engine optimisation, is the same idea aimed at generative chat tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. In a proposal the labels matter less than the deliverables: clear answers, schema, entity coverage, citations, and a report showing which assistants name you. Heavy on the acronym, light on the deliverables, is the tell.

### Does AEO replace SEO or work alongside it?

AEO works alongside SEO; it doesn't replace it. The same pages that answer a question clearly enough to be quoted by an AI assistant also tend to rank in normal search, so the foundations overlap. What changes is the extra layer: tighter answer formatting, heavier schema, deliberate entity coverage, and citation tracking against named prompts. Most Australian agencies now sell both. That's fine, as long as the AEO half is itemised, not folded into content where you can't see it.

### How do I check whether my business already shows up in ChatGPT or Perplexity?

Open ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overview, then ask the three or four questions a real customer would ask before buying your service, with your city in the question. Note whether your business is named, what it says, and whether the facts are right. Do it again next month. That five-minute habit is the cheapest AEO audit there is, the same starting point any decent agency uses. If you're never named and competitors always are, you've found your priority.
