# Copilot vs ChatGPT: Which to Pick If You're on Microsoft 365

> **Quick Answer:** **If your business already pays for Microsoft 365, start with Copilot, then add ChatGPT only for the jobs Copilot can't do.**
> - Copilot reads your emails, files and meetings
> - ChatGPT is stronger for images and deep research
> - Free Copilot Chat is already in your plan
> - Test free before you buy seats

Most Microsoft 365 business plans already include Copilot Chat at no extra cost. That gives you a free test to run before anyone buys a ChatGPT seat.

This guide on Copilot vs ChatGPT compares what each does inside Outlook, Teams and Excel, with Australian prices checked on each vendor's own page on 2 October 2026.

## Copilot vs ChatGPT: What's the Real Difference?

**Copilot and ChatGPT differ mostly in where they work. Copilot lives inside your Microsoft 365 apps and can read your work data, while ChatGPT is a separate app that's stronger on its own.**

Under the hood they're closer than most people think. Microsoft's [Australian Copilot pricing page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-au/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing) says its paid plans run on the "latest OpenAI models: GPT-5.X". That's the same company that makes ChatGPT. So the choice is less about which is smarter and more about where your staff already work.

Copilot's edge is what Microsoft calls **Work IQ**. It means the AI can search your emails, meetings, chats and files to answer you. ChatGPT starts with what you paste in or connect to it. For a business that already runs on Outlook and Teams, that difference decides most of the choice.

## What Can Copilot Do in Outlook, Teams and Excel That ChatGPT Can't?

**Paid Copilot can answer questions from your own mail, meetings, chats and files. It's built into the apps where that work sits.**

Microsoft's [licence comparison page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/how-copilot-chat-works-with-and-without-a-microsoft-365-copilot-license) spells out the split. Without a paid licence, Copilot's work data reasoning is "Limited (uploaded files)". With one, it's "Full (meetings, emails, chats, files, and more)". In Teams, the line is firmer. Microsoft's [Teams chat help page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/copilot-in-teams-chats-2c613de4-cd26-4ae3-9e4b-6905d745d991) says a user "must have a Copilot license" to ask Copilot questions in a chat.

Excel is the weak spot. The Australian Government's [Copilot trial evaluation](https://www.digital.gov.au/initiatives/copilot-trial/microsoft-365-copilot-evaluation-report-full/executive-summary-glossary) found "Poor Excel functionality and access issues in Outlook hampered use". That trial ran from January to June 2024, so Copilot has changed since. Still, test it on your own spreadsheets before you trust it with them. Outlook and Teams are where Copilot earns its seat.

## What Can ChatGPT Do That Copilot Can't?

**ChatGPT does more on its own. It has stronger image tools, deep research and newer models than Copilot gives most small businesses.**

[OpenAI's pricing page](https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing/) lists Plus with "Advanced reasoning models with GPT-6", plus deep research, image creation and scheduled tasks. Copilot's paid plans list GPT-5.X. If your work is writing ads, making social images or researching a new market, ChatGPT usually gets you further in fewer steps.

ChatGPT also isn't locked out of Microsoft. The Business plan says it can "Connect to Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, Microsoft 365, and more", with "No training on your business data by default". It can also search live pages, which is how [ChatGPT searches the web](/blog/does-chatgpt-search-the-web) for current answers. What it can't do is sit inside Outlook and read the thread you're looking at.

## Copilot vs ChatGPT Side by Side for a Small Business

**For a Microsoft 365 business, Copilot wins on your own work data and ChatGPT wins on range.** Every price below is in Australian dollars, checked on 2 October 2026.

| Job or feature | Copilot (Microsoft 365) | ChatGPT |
|---|---|---|
| Summarise an email thread in Outlook | Built in | Paste it in first |
| Ask a question in a Teams chat | Paid licence needed | Not inside Teams |
| Search your own files and meetings | Full with paid licence | Via connectors on Business |
| Create images for ads and social posts | Included, priority on paid plans | More complex and accurate on Plus |
| Deep research on a new market | Researcher agent on paid plans | Expanded on Plus |
| Models named on pricing page | GPT-5.X | GPT-6 on Plus |
| Paid business seat | A$26.91 a month, yearly, plus GST | A$28 a month, yearly |

Copilot is the better pick when the job starts in your inbox, calendar or files. ChatGPT is the better pick when the job starts from a blank page.

## How Much Do Copilot and ChatGPT Cost in Australia?

**Copilot Business costs A$26.91 a user a month on a yearly plan, and ChatGPT Business costs A$28 a seat a month billed yearly.** Both prices come with fine print.

Microsoft's Copilot price is a promotion that runs from 1 July to 31 December 2026, and it applies to the first year only. The full price is A$31.40, or A$37.68 on a monthly plan, and none of these include GST. ChatGPT Business is A$35 a seat on a monthly plan. For one person, ChatGPT Plus is A$30 a month and Go is A$13.

Doing nothing has a price too. Say five staff each get a Copilot seat and a ChatGPT Business seat.

- Copilot and ChatGPT Business seats a person a month, **A$54.91**
- Months a year, **12**
- = Spent a year, **A$3,294.60**

If both tools end up writing the same emails, half of that buys nothing. [What manual processes cost](/blog/how-much-are-manual-processes-costing-your-business) is often the bigger number, but overlapping seats are the easiest leak to see.

## Is Copilot Chat Free With Microsoft 365?

**Yes, Copilot Chat comes free with most Microsoft 365 business plans. So you can test Copilot before you pay for a single seat.**

Microsoft's [business plans page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-au/microsoft-365/business/compare-all-microsoft-365-business-products) says Copilot Chat "is available at no additional cost" to users with an eligible subscription. Even Business Basic, at A$10.50 a user a month, includes web-grounded chat, writing help and data analysis. Copilot in Outlook is included with or without the paid licence, per Microsoft's own licence table.

**A two-week test before you buy paid Copilot seats**

- [ ] Turn on free Copilot Chat for every staff member
- [ ] Use it on real emails and documents for two weeks
- [ ] Note who keeps hitting the uploaded files limit
- [ ] Buy paid seats for those people first

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Copilot just ChatGPT inside Microsoft?**

Copilot is not just ChatGPT inside Microsoft, though both use OpenAI models. Microsoft's Australian pricing page says paid Copilot runs on the "latest OpenAI models: GPT-5.X". The difference is what each can see. Copilot can read your emails, meetings and files through Microsoft 365. ChatGPT is a separate app from OpenAI with its own features, such as stronger image creation and newer GPT-6 reasoning models on paid plans.

**Is ChatGPT safe to use with Microsoft 365 business data?**

ChatGPT can be safe with Microsoft 365 business data if you use a business plan, not a personal account. OpenAI's Business plan states "No training on your business data by default" and adds admin controls and single sign-on. Free and personal plans are riskier. A 2025 survey of over 500 Australian decision-makers found 81% using free AI tools did so with confidential information, as [AAP reported](https://aapnews.aap.com.au/news/aussie-businesses-are-risking-data-in-race-to-use-ai).

**Can I use ChatGPT inside Outlook or Excel?**

You can't use ChatGPT natively inside Outlook the way Copilot works, but you can connect them. OpenAI's pricing page lists Excel, Word and PowerPoint extensions among its features. The Business plan can also connect to Microsoft 365, so it can work with your files. Copilot still has the edge for an email thread you have open, because it's built into Outlook itself rather than added on.

**Do small businesses in Australia actually use Copilot and ChatGPT?**

Australian small businesses use both tools widely, though few have a plan for them. A 2025 national survey of 1,042 decision-makers found [92% of businesses using ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot and similar platforms](https://www.researchsociety.com.au/news-item/20353/smes-race-into-ai-without-strategy-or-roadmaps). The same survey found 76% had no clear AI strategy. That gap is why so many teams pay for two tools doing one job. A short written rule on which tool does which job closes most of it.

**Is Microsoft 365 Copilot worth it for a small business?**

Microsoft 365 Copilot is worth it for a small business when staff spend hours each day in email, meetings and documents. Australia's 2024 government trial found time savings of around an hour a day on summarising, first drafts and information searches. Those gains landed in only a few tasks, though. Test the free Copilot Chat first, then pay for seats where the gain is clear.
