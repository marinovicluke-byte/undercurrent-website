# Which Is the Best AI Model for a Small Business in 2026?

> **Quick Answer:** **The best AI model for a small business is the one that suits the job, not the brand with the most hype.**
> - Claude Opus 5.5 for building workflows and hard thinking
> - Claude Sonnet 5.5 for jobs that repeat all day
> - Copilot if you live in Microsoft 365
> - Gemini if you live in Gmail

It's Monday morning and three of your staff are paying for three different AI tools. One swears by ChatGPT, one by Claude, and the bookkeeper uses whatever came with Outlook. Nobody can say which one is actually better for quotes, emails or the end-of-month report.

This guide gives you one table of five models against the jobs a small business really has, with prices checked on each vendor's own page on 2 October 2026.

## What Is the Best AI Model for a Small Business?

**There is no single best AI model for a small business. The best one is the model that does your most common job well, inside the software you already pay for.**

Most owners pick by brand, then force every job through it. That's backwards. Writing a quote, sorting an inbox and building a custom app are different jobs, and the models are priced and built for different work. Anthropic itself describes [Claude Sonnet 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/sonnet) as "a faster, lower-cost complement to Opus 5.5" for "well-scoped everyday tasks". That's the vendor telling you to pick by job.

UnderCurrent Automations uses Claude right now. We're open to changing, but Claude works for us, and swapping models every month wastes time. Our advice to owners is simple. Use Opus to build, Sonnet to run the job every day, and the assistant inside your email suite for everything your staff do by hand.

## Which AI Model Should You Use for Each Job?

**Match the model to the job, then check the price on the vendor's own page, because both change often.** Every price below was checked on 2 October 2026 and is in Australian dollars unless marked.

| Job | Best pick | Why | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build a workflow or app | Claude Opus 5.5 | Strongest Claude for coding and agents | Claude Pro A$34/month |
| Repeat job, like sorting email | Claude Sonnet 5.5 | Half the Opus API price | US$2 in, US$10 out per million tokens |
| Work in Microsoft 365 | Copilot | Inside Outlook, Word and Excel | A$26.91/user/month yearly, plus GST |
| Work in Gmail and Docs | Gemini | Built into Google Workspace | Business Standard A$19.80/user/month |
| All-round assistant | ChatGPT Plus | Writing, images and voice | A$30/month |
| Tight budget | Gemini AI Plus or ChatGPT Go | Cheapest paid tiers | A$7.99 or A$13/month |

When it's fixed, each job has one tool. Staff draft in the assistant inside their email suite, and the repeat jobs run on their own in a workflow.

## When Is Claude Opus 5.5 Worth Paying For Over Sonnet?

**Claude Opus 5.5 is worth it when the job is long, messy or hard to check. Sonnet 5.5 is the better buy when the job is clear and repeats.**

Anthropic lists [Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/opus) at US$4 per million input tokens and US$20 per million output tokens. A **token** is a chunk of text about three-quarters of a word long, and it's how AI usage is billed. Sonnet 5.5 costs exactly half that. On a workflow that runs thousands of times a month, half adds up fast.

At UnderCurrent Automations, Opus does the building and the hard judgement calls. We run Sonnet on the repeat jobs once the rules are clear. Both come with a [Claude Pro plan](https://claude.com/pricing) at A$34 a month, or A$28 a month billed yearly, so one person can try both before you pay for API use. Our [guide to using Claude in your business](/blog/how-to-use-claude-in-your-business-australian-smb-guide) covers the setup.

## Gemini vs ChatGPT: Which Is Better for Your Business?

**Gemini is the better pick if your business runs on Gmail and Google Docs. ChatGPT is the better pick for one person who wants a strong all-round assistant.**

Gemini's edge is where it lives. Google's [Australian Workspace pricing page](https://workspace.google.com/intl/en_au/pricing) shows Gemini in Gmail even on Business Starter, and in Docs and Meet from Business Standard. Your staff don't need a new app or a new login. On its own, [Google AI Pro](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/) is A$32.99 a month, with 4x the usage of the free tier.

ChatGPT wins on range. [OpenAI's pricing page](https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing/) lists Plus at A$30 a month, with GPT-6 reasoning models, deep research, image creation and scheduled tasks. For a team, ChatGPT Business is A$35 a seat a month, or A$28 billed yearly. Search comes with every plan, so it can check live pages, which is how [ChatGPT searches the web](/blog/does-chatgpt-search-the-web) for current answers.

## Should You Use Copilot if You're on Microsoft 365?

**Yes, start with Copilot if your business already runs on Microsoft 365. It works inside the apps your staff open every day, so there's less to learn.**

Microsoft's [Australian Copilot pricing page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-au/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing) lists Microsoft 365 Copilot Business at A$26.91 a user a month on a yearly plan. That price is a promotion that runs to 31 December 2026, and the monthly plan is A$37.68. Both exclude GST. Microsoft also says Copilot Chat is free to try with a work account, so test it before you buy seats.

Under the hood, Microsoft says paid plans use the "latest OpenAI models: GPT-5.X". So Copilot runs on the same family of model as ChatGPT, just placed inside Outlook and Excel. The catch is that staff still need to know how to ask it well, which is where [AI training for small business](/blog/ai-training-australia-small-business-guide) pays off.

**UnderCurrent Automations runs its own email sorting workflow on Claude, the same model it uses for everything else. Sticking with one model that works beats chasing every new release.**

So we built an [email sorting workflow](/automation) that reads each new email, labels it and flags the ones that matter. ## Why the Best AI Model Changes Every Month

**The best AI model changes often because vendors ship new versions within days of each other. Any ranking older than a month is probably out of date.**

Anthropic's own pages date Opus 5.5 to 22 September 2026 and Sonnet 5.5 to 28 September 2026, six days apart. Google's [Gemini model page](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/) leads with Gemini 4 Argon. Yet its Australian plans page still lists 3.6 Flash and 3.1 Pro for the Gemini app. The name in the news isn't always the model your plan gives you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Claude better than Gemini for business?**

Claude is better than Gemini for building workflows and handling long, careful writing, while Gemini is better if your business runs on Gmail and Google Docs. Claude Opus 5.5 is Anthropic's strongest model for coding and agents. Gemini's advantage is that it sits inside Google Workspace, so staff use it without a new app. Pick Claude for building and judgement, and pick Gemini for everyday work inside Google's own tools.

**What AI model does Microsoft Copilot use?**

Microsoft 365 Copilot uses OpenAI's models, which Microsoft lists as the "latest OpenAI models: GPT-5.X" on its Australian pricing page. That means Copilot is the same family of model as ChatGPT, placed inside Outlook, Word, Excel and Teams. The difference is where it works, not how smart it is. If your staff already live in Microsoft 365, Copilot saves them switching between apps.

**Is the free version of ChatGPT good enough for a small business?**

The free version of ChatGPT is fine for testing ideas, but it is limited for daily business work. OpenAI's pricing page lists free access to GPT-5.6 Luna, with limited uploads, image creation and deep research. Paid Plus at A$30 a month adds GPT-6 reasoning models and scheduled tasks. For customer details, use a business plan with an admin, not a free personal account.

**Is ChatGPT Go worth it for a small business?**

ChatGPT Go is worth it for one person who has outgrown the free plan but doesn't need advanced reasoning. OpenAI lists Go at A$13 a month in Australia, with more messages, uploads and image creation than free. OpenAI also says Go may include ads. For a team, or for work with customer data, ChatGPT Business is the safer choice at A$28 a seat billed yearly.

**Is Gemini free with Google Workspace?**

Gemini is included with Google Workspace business plans, so you don't pay for it separately. Google's Australian pricing page shows Gemini in Gmail and the Gemini app on Business Starter. Business Standard adds Gemini in Docs, Meet and more, at A$19.80 a user a month. A personal Google account gets Gemini free too, but business data belongs in a Workspace account your admin controls.
